Hi, I'm Travis — a Senior SDET who loves treating test automation like a product. I've spent the last several years building reliable end-to-end and contract test suites with **Playwright**, **Cypress**, and **Pact**, mentoring engineers on testing strategy, and shipping internal tooling that makes QA work less painful and more measurable.

I write about the day-to-day of this work on [the blog]({{ '/blog/' | relative_url }}) — what I'm building, what I'm reading, what's not working, and what finally clicks. The [career]({{ '/career/' | relative_url }}) page has the longer story of how I got here, and you can find my hands-on test automation work in [my projects on GitHub](https://github.com/travwritescode/test-portfolio).

If you want to chat about test automation, QA strategy, hiring SDETs, or anything in between, the easiest way to reach me is by email or [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/travis-anderson-sdet).
