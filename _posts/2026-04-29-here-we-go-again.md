After nearly two years at Giv, my time came to an end unexpectedly. I was laid off from my position as the company's sole SDET as engineering leadership decided to "go lean" without QA due to their current stage as a startup.

This one hurt considerably more than any other previous layoff. I enjoyed working with all of my coworkers, I had ownership over the entire testing platform, and I was hitting my highest productivity and output of my career. I had a well-defined direction and was sprinting ahead. I was excited about what I was building and I was bought in to the company vision.

Then, I was hit with the news at 9:30am on Monday morning. The company had to make some difficult staffing decisions and leadership was cutting the QA function (me). I won't go into the details of why eliminating QA in a healthcare SaaS startup is very dangerous, just to say that *it is*.

I took the day to come to terms with my new status of unemployed, and pretty quickly realized I needed something to direct my energy toward. I went from creative output *daily* to not having anywhere to direct my energy. I needed to build, not just to keep my skills sharp or to keep learning, but because it is *what I do*.

So if you're here, welcome. I encourage you to check out my [GitHub profile](https://github.com/travwritescode) where I am building a to-do list app and the full testing stack for it. I plan to keep building on top of it to emulate a real enterprise development environment, but also because I am personally a little dissatisfied with the offerings for web-based productivity apps.

I'm having fun with it so far. I have the [front end](https://github.com/travwritescode/flowstate-ui), [back end](https://github.com/travwritescode/flowstate-api), and [API test framework](https://github.com/travwritescode/pytest-api-framework) stood up. Follow along and see what else I can do!
